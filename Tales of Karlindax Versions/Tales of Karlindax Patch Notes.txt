29/may/2026
0.2.0 Inventory Update
- Player can now pick up items (loot) and save them in an inventory.
- Added items: Rusty Sword / Old Wooden Shield / Shortbow / Arrow / Worn-out robes / Small potion
- Player can now open and close the inventory, and also check the full info of a selected item.
- Ammo and consumable items can be stacked.
2/jun/2026
0.3.0 Equipment Update
- Player can now equip up to 4 items (mainhand, offhand, armor set and consumable item) which are shown in inventory
- Equipped items now show up on the player's model (using placeholder models).
- Attacking with a melee weapon now uses said weapon's hitbox, instead of an "attack object" appearing on air.
- Attacking with a bow now shoots an arrow and removes one arrow from inventory.
- Weapons' base and backstab damage are now multiplied by the wielder's attack stat.
- Before this update, a regular attack always did 1 damage and a backstab did 2 damage. Now damage depends on the weapon and wielder.
- Skeleton enemy's health increased from 2 to 10. Player's health increased from 3 to 10.
- Skeleton enemy now wields a Rusty Sword instead of attacking with an "attack object" appearing on air.
- Skeleton enemy's attack stat is 0.8. Player's is 1.
- Before this update, if you were hit while blocking with a shield you lost 0.5 health. Now damage is reduced according
to the shield's damage reduction stat instead.
- Blocking with a shield and attacking with a weapon consumes 1 durability.
- Wearing armor reduces damage according to its damage reduction stat when hit while not blocking with a shield. Unlike
shields, armors can reduce backstab damage.
- Receiving an attack while not blocking with a shield reduces armor's durability by 1.
- When an item's durability reaches 0, it is destroyed.
- Rusty Sword's ID changed from 0 to 99 to prevent a bug of it not showing up on inventory.
- Player can now use consumable items, like potions.
3/jun/2026
0.3.1 Stats Update
- You can now check your stats on the left part of the inventory.
- Player now has a name, a level, experience, and experience to level up.
- Player stamina regen now depends on a "stamina regen speed" stat, which base value is 0.2/second (the default value
prior to this update).
- Player has now a stat to measure the full weight of your equipment.
- Player has now a money stat.
- Player speed is now reduced by the weight of your equipment.
- Enemies now drop loot upon death. They drop experience, money, and items.
- Skeleton enemy drops: 2 experience, 1 coin, Rusty Sword.

19/jun/2026
0.4.0 Big Skull Update
- Added new item: Cloth Robes (a better version of the Worn-out robes, slightly heavier)
- Player can no longer carry more than 8 items (the amount of slots shown in inventory)
- Scenario updated.
- Added new enemy: Skeleton Dressed (is a regular Skeleton but wearing Worn-out robes, so it has a 0.1 damage reduction bonus)
- Skeleton Dressed drops Worn-out robes, 3 xp and 2 coins.
- From now on, some enemies will have a damage reduction bonus according to their armor, tough skin, or other reasons.
- Added new enemy: Skeleton Shield (is a regular Skeleton but using an Old Wooden Shield, so it has a 0.1 shield stat)
- Skeleton Shield drops Old Wooden Shield, 2 xp and 2 coins.
- From now on, some enemies will have the ability to block attacks periodically, using a shield stat to reduce damage proportionally.
- Added new enemy: Skeleton Boss (equipped with Shortsword, Wooden Shield and Cloth Robes) (twice as big as regular Skeleton)
- Skeleton Boss stats: 1.2 attack, 15 health, 0.2 damage reduction, 0.2 shield
- Skeleton Boss drops Shortsword, 10 xp and 5 coins.
- Enemies now patrol between 2 way points in a straight line instead of moving randomly.
- Fixed enemies to use the proper damage of their weapon.
- Enemies no longer react to attacks when they are dead. Dead enemies are no longer solid.
- Fixed a bug that sometimes made loot not disappear upon picking up, thus making it infinite.
- Player's rotation speed has increased from 45º to 90º.
- Fixed a bug that caused dead enemies to loop between blocking and dying animation.
- Skeleton Boss size adjusted to 1.5 times the size of a regular Skeleton to prevent it from getting stuck in wall archs sometimes.
- Fixed a bug that didn't update the inventory properly when opened for the first time.
